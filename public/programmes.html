After delivery

Join our postpartum package.

Your body has done something huge. Rebuild gently from home — on your baby’s schedule, not a gym’s. Start whenever your doctor clears you to exercise; there’s no fixed intake date.

  • ✓Pelvic floor reactivation — breathing and gentle activation to rebuild from the inside out.

Core healing

Join our diastasis recti class.

Still have a “mummy tummy”, a gap down your middle, or back pain months after delivery? This class focuses on diastasis recti and the pelvic floor — slow, controlled work that brings your core back together safely.

Not sure if you have diastasis recti? We check for it during your fitness assessment.
