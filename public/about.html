Fitness that fits your life — and respects your culture.

Cyber Fitness is an online platform for home-based fitness: live classes with certified trainers and nutrition guidance from clinical dietitians, without travelling to a gym.

Our mission

To empower individuals and communities to achieve sustainable health, physical strength and mental well-being through inclusive, culturally conscious, evidence-based programs.

Our vision

A global community where wellness is accessible, equitable and culturally relevant — in line with UN Sustainable Development Goal 3.

The founder’s story

Hijab Wasif believes health is a human right.

Hijab is a certified strength and conditioning coach and the CEO & founder of Cyber Fitness. Her work focuses on UN Sustainable Development Goal 3 — Good Health & Well-Being — serving women, youth, corporate professionals and underserved communities.

Her experience showed her that fitness changes mindsets and lives, not just bodies. So she built programs that combine training with nutrition awareness, stress management and lifestyle change — and partners with NGOs, schools and community groups to make wellness affordable and culturally sensitive.
