Questions

Good to know before you join.

Something else on your mind? Ask us

Do I have to turn my camera on?–

No — the camera is optional in many classes, and women’s batches are led by female trainers.

How many people are in each class?+

Each class is limited to 10–20 members so your coach can give you real attention.

What if a class is full?+

Join the waiting list and you’ll be enrolled automatically if a spot opens.

What equipment do I need?+

Many classes are bodyweight only. We recommend a mat, a pair of dumbbells and a resistance band.

Which time zones do you cover?+

Classes run morning and evening, with times for Pakistan, India, KSA, UAE, the UK and the US. Bookings adjust to your time zone.

Is my payment secure?+

Yes. Payments go through PCI-compliant processors (Stripe or PayPal) with SSL encryption, and we never store card numbers.
